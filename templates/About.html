About GoPredict

GoPredict is an interactive football prediction platform built for fans who enjoy testing their knowledge, intuition, and passion for the beautiful game.

Users predict match scores before kickoff and earn points based on prediction accuracy. These points determine rankings on a live leaderboard, allowing users to compete against other football enthusiasts in a fair and transparent way.

Our mission is to create an engaging and competitive environment that blends strategy, excitement, and community — all powered by real match data and clear, consistent scoring rules.

GoPredict is continuously evolving, with new features, competitions, and leagues being introduced to improve the experience and keep the platform exciting for every user.
