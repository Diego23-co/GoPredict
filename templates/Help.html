Help & FAQ

How do I make predictions?

Visit the Matches page, select the score you think will happen before kickoff, and submit your prediction. Points are awarded based on accuracy.

How is the leaderboard calculated?

Points are awarded for correct predictions. The leaderboard updates automatically as matches finish.

Can I edit my predictions?

Predictions can only be changed before the match starts. Once a match begins, submissions are locked.

Who can I contact for more help?

Use our Contact Us form below to send a message. Our support team will get back to you promptly.
